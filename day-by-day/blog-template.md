### Notes for Use
- **Daily vs. Weekly**: Use the full template for weekly posts (500-800 words) or a shorter version for daily posts (200-300 words). For daily GitHub notes, focus on “What I Learned” and “Challenges” in the repo, saving full posts for weekly blogs.
- **GitHub**: Save each post as a Markdown file in your repo (e.g., `docs/day-01.md`) and commit daily. Link to these in your blog platform.
- **Images**: Host screenshots on GitHub or a free service like Imgur and embed them with `![Alt text](image-url)`.
- **Adaptation**: After 10 days, tweak the template based on your style or audience feedback (e.g., add a “Tool of the Day” section).
